Bacancy Technology - Employee Leave Policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Policy Version: 3.2

1. Annual Leave (Privilege Leave)

All confirmed full-time employees are entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave is accrued at the rate of 1.75 days per month of service. Employees who join mid-year will receive prorated leave based on their date of joining.

Annual leave requests must be submitted at least 7 working days in advance through the HR portal. For leave exceeding 5 consecutive days, requests must be submitted at least 15 working days in advance. All annual leave is subject to manager approval and team workload considerations.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any leave balance exceeding 5 days at the end of the calendar year will be forfeited. There is no encashment of unused annual leave except at the time of separation from the company.

2. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave is credited in full at the beginning of each calendar year. New joiners receive prorated sick leave based on their joining date.

For sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days, employees must submit a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner within 3 working days of returning to work. Failure to provide a medical certificate may result in the leave being treated as unpaid leave.

Sick leave cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year. Unused sick leave will lapse at the end of each calendar year with no encashment.

3. Casual Leave

Employees are entitled to 7 days of casual leave per calendar year. Casual leave is intended for unforeseen or urgent personal matters. A maximum of 3 consecutive days of casual leave can be taken at a time.

Casual leave cannot be combined with any other type of leave. Casual leave requests should be submitted on the same day or the next working day. Prior approval is required when casual leave is foreseeable.

Casual leave cannot be carried forward and will lapse at the end of the calendar year.

4. Maternity Leave

Female employees who have worked for at least 80 days in the 12 months preceding the expected date of delivery are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. For employees who already have 2 or more surviving children, maternity leave is limited to 12 weeks.

Maternity leave can begin up to 8 weeks before the expected date of delivery. The remaining leave must be taken after delivery. Maternity leave also applies in cases of miscarriage (6 weeks) and medical termination of pregnancy (6 weeks from the date of the event).

Employees on maternity leave are entitled to receive their full salary and benefits during the leave period. The company also provides a work-from-home option for up to 4 weeks after the maternity leave period ends, subject to manager approval and role suitability.

5. Paternity Leave

Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 3 months of the child's birth. Paternity leave can be taken in a maximum of 2 installments. Prior approval from the manager is required, and the leave request must be submitted at least 2 weeks before the expected date.

6. Bereavement Leave

In the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, or parent-in-law), employees are entitled to 5 working days of paid bereavement leave. For extended family members (grandparent, grandchild, uncle, aunt), employees are entitled to 3 working days of paid bereavement leave.

Bereavement leave must be taken within 30 days of the family member's passing. Employees should notify their manager as soon as possible and submit a leave request within 3 working days of returning to work.

7. Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)

Employees who work on weekends or public holidays at the request of their manager are entitled to compensatory time off. Comp-off must be availed within 30 days of the extra working day. Comp-off requests require prior approval from the reporting manager.

A maximum of 2 comp-off days can be accumulated at any given time. Comp-off days that are not availed within 30 days will lapse automatically.

8. Public Holidays

The company observes 12 public holidays per calendar year. The list of public holidays is published by the HR department at the beginning of each calendar year. Regional offices may have a different holiday list based on local observances, with the total number remaining at 12.

Employees required to work on a public holiday are entitled to compensatory off as described in Section 7.

9. Leave Without Pay (LWP)

When an employee has exhausted all paid leave balances, additional leave may be taken as Leave Without Pay subject to manager and HR approval. LWP will result in a proportional salary deduction for the days of absence.

Extended LWP exceeding 30 consecutive days may affect the employee's annual increment, bonus eligibility, and other benefits. LWP exceeding 90 consecutive days may be treated as voluntary resignation unless prior written approval has been obtained from the HR Head.

10. General Leave Rules

- Leave is calculated based on working days (Monday to Friday). Weekends and public holidays falling within a leave period are excluded from the leave count.
- Employees on probation are eligible for sick leave and casual leave only. Annual leave eligibility begins after confirmation.
- Half-day leave is available for annual leave and casual leave. The minimum leave unit is half a day.
- Leave cannot be taken during the notice period without explicit written approval from the HR department.
- Misuse of leave or providing false information in a leave request may result in disciplinary action, including termination.
- All leave records are maintained in the HR portal. Employees are responsible for keeping their leave records up to date.

For any questions regarding this policy, please contact the HR department at hr@bacancy.com or raise a ticket on the HR portal.
